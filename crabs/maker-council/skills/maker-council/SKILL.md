# maker-council

No state, no schedule. Invoke: `/maker-council <the decision or question>`.

## Session modes

| Mode | Seats | When |
|---|---|---|
| Quick take | 1 | "What would the leverage lens say about this?" - one named member |
| Council session (default) | 3-5 | A real decision that benefits from conflicting lenses |
| Full council | 6-8 | Bet-the-company calls; long output, offer only when stakes justify |

## The bench

Eight operator lenses, chosen so they collide. Seat by collision, not by fame:

- **Calm company**: profitability over growth, small teams, no VC
- **First principles**: delete the part, maniacal urgency
- **Day 1**: one-way vs two-way doors, customer obsession, long-term
- **Flat org**: mission as the boss, strategic pain tolerance
- **Big bets**: creative risk, brand stewardship
- **Startup craft**: do things that do not scale, default alive
- **Leverage**: specific knowledge, productize yourself
- **Bootstrapped resourcefulness**: sell first, own everything

## Run

1. **Clarify once**: the question, context (stage, revenue shape, runway, what was tried - check memory before asking), stakes.
2. **Seat the council**: pick 3-5 whose lenses genuinely conflict on THIS question. Say who is seated and why.
3. **Each take**: their position on the question, grounded in their documented frameworks and stated positions, 3-5 sentences, plus the strongest objection to another member's take.
4. **Disagreement round**: name the real trade-off the council is fighting over. One lens is usually wrong for this stage - say which and why.
5. **Synthesis**: what changed after hearing everyone, the recommendation, and what would flip it.

## Rules

- Label the output as persona simulation in one line. Takes are grounded in documented positions, never invented quotes.
- The value is the disagreement. If all seats agree, you seated wrong - reseat once with a genuine contrarian lens.
- Committing to a direction is the user's call: the council surfaces angles, it does not decide.
